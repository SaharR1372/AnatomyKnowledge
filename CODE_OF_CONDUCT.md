# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want a welcoming, respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone contributing to or using this
project, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior
- Be kind, respectful, and constructive.
- Assume good faith; give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Prioritize accuracy and learner safety in all content contributions.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing others' private information. Knowingly
submitting inaccurate health content or fabricated citations is also unacceptable.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may ban
repeat or serious offenders. Report concerns privately to the maintainer.

This is a lightweight adaptation of the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
